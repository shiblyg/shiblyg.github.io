Research experience

Research Assistant, Vision Lab

Aug 2018 - present

Old Dominion University · projects funded by NIH / NIBIB

  • Adult-to-pediatric domain shift (MF-DNN). Multiresolution fractal context encoding with a gradient-reversal domain-alignment module and Monte-Carlo dropout uncertainty; transferred from 1,251 adult glioma cases to 228 pediatric cases, reaching DSC 0.879 (whole tumor) and 0.808 (tumor core) on the BraTS-PEDs 2023 validation portal.
  • Brain tumor segmentation (UNCE-NODE). Neural ODE-enhanced U-Net with continuous-depth modeling and shared weights across integration steps; ~30% fewer trainable parameters and faster inference at preserved Dice accuracy, with uncertainty quantification for clinical reliability.
  • Uncertainty-aware multi-organ segmentation. Ensemble nnU-Nets with 5-fold cross-validation combining entropy-based total uncertainty and mutual-information epistemic uncertainty; estimates reliably stratify erroneous segmentations for targeted clinical error analysis.
  • Recurrence vs. radiation necrosis, and survival. Multiresolution radiomic features with repeated random sub-sampling reached AUC 0.892 ± 0.055; a copula-based survival pipeline addressing dependent censoring reached cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.77, outperforming Cox regression.
  • Functional brain networks in opioid use disorder. Time-frequency features from rs-fMRI BOLD signals with random forest and Boruta analysis to identify networks with high discriminative power for OUD vs. healthy controls.
  • Learnability under sampling noise. Resolvable Expressive Capacity framework with eigentask decomposition, plus a Bayesian inference framework for noise-aware digital twins of tumor evolution.
