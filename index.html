Newest · SPIE Optics + Photonics 2026

Adult-to-pediatric domain shift in brain tumor segmentation

Pediatric brain tumor datasets are orders of magnitude smaller than adult ones, and pediatric gliomas differ in anatomy, location, and enhancement — so a model fit on adults transfers badly. MF-DNN answers this with multiresolution fractal descriptors concatenated at the encoder bottleneck, a gradient-reversal domain-alignment module that pushes features the adult-vs-pediatric classifier cannot separate, and Monte-Carlo dropout for voxel-level epistemic uncertainty that flags low-confidence regions for radiologist review.

Presented at SPIE Optics + Photonics 2026

Talk on adult-to-pediatric domain shift in brain tumor segmentation using multiresolution fractal deep neural networks — a gradient-reversal domain adaptation module that pushes the encoder toward domain-invariant features when pediatric data is scarce. Trained on 1,251 adult glioma cases and transferred to 228 pediatric cases, the model reaches DSC 0.879 (whole tumor) and 0.808 (tumor core) on the BraTS-PEDs 2023 validation portal. With A. Temtam and K. M. Iftekharuddin, ODU Vision Lab.
