Research

Brain Tumor Recurrence Classification & Survival Prediction

NIH/NBIB Funded

Distinguishing tumor recurrence from radiation necrosis presents a critical clinical challenge due to similar imaging characteristics. I developed a radiomics-based model using multiresolution features and repeated random sub-sampling, achieving AUC = 0.892 ± 0.055.

For survival prediction, I applied copula-based censoring models to construct a prognostic index, achieving cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.77, outperforming traditional Cox regression and improving patient risk stratification.

Brain Tumor Segmentation using Neural ODEs

NIH/NBIB Funded | MICCAI BraTS Challenge

I developed UNCE-NODE, a Neural ODE-enhanced U-Net framework with continuous-depth modeling. This architecture reduces trainable parameters by ~30% while maintaining segmentation accuracy through shared weights across integration steps.

Applied uncertainty quantification improves clinical reliability. Participated in MICCAI BraTS challenges (2021-2023), contributing to advancing automated brain tumor analysis.

Uncertainty-Aware Multi-Organ Segmentation

AMOS Challenge 2022 - 8th Place

Medical image segmentation models often fail silently without indicating prediction confidence. I designed an uncertainty quantification framework combining ensemble nnU-Nets with 5-fold cross-validation.

By quantifying total uncertainty (entropy) and epistemic uncertainty (mutual information), the framework reliably stratifies erroneous segmentations, enabling targeted clinical review and improving model interpretability through class activation mapping.

Functional Brain Network Analysis in Opioid Use Disorder

NIH Funded

I extracted time-frequency features from resting-state fMRI BOLD signals to identify aberrant neural activity within functional networks. Applied Random Forest modeling learns discriminative patterns for classifying opioid use disorder versus healthy controls.

Through multiple ML methods (Random Forest, Boruta, statistical analyses), I identified functional networks with high discriminative power, contributing to understanding neurobiological mechanisms of addiction.
