Research Interests

My work focuses on addressing fundamental questions in biomedical imaging and computational modeling

Biomedical Image Analysis

Multi-modal and 3D volumetric MRI, functional MRI, and CT imaging. Specializing in registration, segmentation, and classification using physics-informed modeling (PDE), radiomics feature extraction, longitudinal disease progression modeling, uncertainty quantification, and explainable AI.

Computer Vision & Deep Learning

Advanced deep learning and classical image processing for biological structure detection. Automated segmentation and predictive modeling using U-Net, nnU-Net, Neural ODEs, uncertainty-aware frameworks, and generative models for medical imaging applications.

Data Mining & Survival Analysis

Survival and risk prediction using imaging biomarkers, prognostic modeling, inferential statistical modeling for interpretability, feature selection, and class-imbalanced data analysis. Focus on translating computational findings to clinical applications.

Research Projects

Current and ongoing research initiatives

Brain Tumor Recurrence vs. Radiation Necrosis Classification

NIH/NBIB Funded | Ongoing

Clinical Challenge: Distinguishing tumor recurrence from radiation-induced necrosis in post-treatment MRI scans using multiresolution radiomic features.

Results: Achieved AUC = 0.892 ± 0.055 with copula-based survival models for improved risk stratification.

📄 IEEE JBHI 2024

Brain Tumor Segmentation using Neural ODEs (UNCE-NODE)

NIH/NBIB Funded | MICCAI BraTS Challenge

Innovation: Neural ODE-enhanced U-Net framework with continuous-depth modeling, reducing parameters by ~30% while preserving segmentation accuracy.

Impact: Participated in MICCAI BraTS challenges (2021-2023) with uncertainty quantification for clinical reliability.

📄 MICCAI 2022

Uncertainty-Aware Multi-Organ Segmentation

Ongoing | AMOS Challenge 2022 - 8th Place

Framework: Ensemble nnU-Nets with uncertainty quantification combining total uncertainty (entropy) and epistemic uncertainty (mutual information).

Results: Demonstrated uncertainty estimates reliably stratify erroneous segmentations with class activation mapping for interpretability.

📄 SPIE Medical Imaging 2023
🔬

Functional Brain Network Identification in Opioid Use Disorder

NIH Funded | Ongoing

Objective: Identify aberrant functional brain networks associated with opioid use disorder (OUD) using resting-state fMRI.

Methodology: Extracted time-frequency features from rs-fMRI BOLD signals to localize neural activity patterns within functional networks. Applied Random Forest modeling to learn discriminative patterns for classifying OUD vs. healthy controls.

Feature Selection: Employed multiple ML methods (Random Forest, Boruta, statistical analyses) to evaluate feature significance and identify functional networks with high discriminative power.

Impact: Findings contribute to understanding neurobiological mechanisms of addiction and potential biomarkers for treatment response.

📄 Computers in Biology and Medicine 2025

Glioblastoma Survival Prediction using Copula Models

NIH/NBIB Funded | Published

Innovation: Copula-based survival analysis addressing dependent censoring with prognostic index from radiomic features.

Results: Cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.77, outperforming Cox regression with improved patient risk stratification into high/low risk groups.

📄 IEEE JBHI 2024

Functional Brain Network Analysis in Opioid Use Disorder

NIH Funded | Ongoing

Objective: Identify aberrant functional brain networks in opioid use disorder using rs-fMRI BOLD signals and machine learning.

Methods: Time-frequency feature extraction with Random Forest classification to distinguish OUD vs. healthy controls.

Slot-Loaded Microstrip Patch Antenna Design

Completed (M.S. Thesis 2016)

Objective: Design and fabricate a slot-loaded microstrip patch antenna operating at 2.45 GHz for wireless communication applications.

Methodology: Conducted electromagnetic simulation, design optimization, fabrication, and experimental validation. Built comprehensive simulation and measurement capabilities for the lab.
