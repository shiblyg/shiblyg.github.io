Research Projects

Current and ongoing research initiatives

Brain Tumor Recurrence Classification & Survival Prediction

NIH/NBIB Funded | Ongoing

Clinical Challenge: Distinguishing tumor recurrence from radiation necrosis and predicting patient survival using multiresolution radiomic features.

Classification: Achieved AUC = 0.892 ± 0.055 distinguishing recurrent tumor from radiation necrosis.

Survival: Copula-based models achieved AUC ≈ 0.77, outperforming Cox regression for patient risk stratification.

📄 IEEE JBHI 2024

Brain Tumor Segmentation using Neural ODEs (UNCE-NODE)

NIH/NBIB Funded | MICCAI BraTS Challenge

Innovation: Neural ODE-enhanced U-Net framework with continuous-depth modeling, reducing trainable parameters by ~30% while preserving Dice accuracy.

Impact: Participated in MICCAI BraTS challenges (2021-2023) with uncertainty quantification for clinical reliability.

📄 MICCAI 2022

Uncertainty-Aware Multi-Organ Segmentation

Ongoing | AMOS Challenge 2022 - 8th Place

Framework: Ensemble nnU-Nets with 5-fold cross-validation combining total uncertainty (entropy) and epistemic uncertainty (mutual information).

Results: Uncertainty estimates reliably stratify erroneous segmentations with class activation mapping for improved interpretability.

📄 SPIE Medical Imaging 2023

Functional Brain Network Analysis in Opioid Use Disorder

NIH Funded | Ongoing

Objective: Identify aberrant functional brain networks in opioid use disorder using resting-state fMRI BOLD signals.

Methods: Time-frequency feature extraction with Random Forest modeling to classify OUD vs. healthy controls, identifying functional networks with high discriminative power.

📄 Computers in Biology and Medicine 2025
🧪

Physics-Informed Tumor Growth Modeling with Uncertainty

Ongoing

Challenge: PDE-based tumor growth models face constraints from sampling noise, limiting predictive reliability for patient-specific digital twins.

Innovation: Proposed Resolvable Expressive Capacity (REC) framework with eigentask decomposition. Developed Bayesian inference framework for noise-aware digital twin models of tumor evolution.

Completed (M.S. Thesis 2016)

Objective: Design and fabricate slot-loaded microstrip patch antenna at 2.45 GHz for wireless communication.

Contribution: Built simulation capability and experimental setup for the Microwave & Optical Fiber Communications Lab at University of Dhaka.
